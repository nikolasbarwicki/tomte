# 06: Login walls are "source unavailable"; bot walls are waited out and handed off

Superseded in part: vault removed (`.scratch/remove-credential-vault/`, 2026-11-17). A login wall no longer depends on vault coverage: in chat the note asks for a takeover, in a routine it is always "source unavailable".

**What to build:** The browser tools recognise two kinds of page wall and tell the bot what to do, with no site named in the code. A login wall on an origin where the bot has no granted vault entry is "source unavailable": the run skips the source, says so in its report and goes on, without parking. A bot wall (Cloudflare, DataDome, PerimeterX, Imperva) is waited out for a few seconds, and if it stays, handed to me as a takeover, which parks in a routine. See the spec section "Walls" and Decision 5 in `docs/plans/grok-bot-replacement-roadmap.md`.

## Comments

- Deviation: the fake engine's per-call snapshots are `pages[url].later` (snapshots after the first; the last repeats), not `then`: oxlint's `unicorn/no-thenable` rejects a `then` key. The header comment of `testing/fake-agent-browser.ts` documents it; `tab list` reports the title of the latest view, `open` and a navigating action restart the count.
- `login.ts` change: `FIELD` and `PASSWORD_FIELD` are exported so `page-walls.ts` shares them, and `BrowserLogin.coverage(botId, origin)` is the one new function. The match is the exact origin: the engine's page `origin` is normalised with `new URL(x).origin` (the real engine may report a URL), and a page whose origin cannot be read gets no login note.
- The wait is `WALL_RECHECKS = 3` looks `wallPauseMs` apart (default 2 s, about 6 s in all against the 30 s lane wait). `wallPauseMs` is a `BrowserRuntimeOptions` field (like `laneWaitMs`), carried through `createBrowser` and the endpoint to `BrowserToolContext`; tests set it to 0. A wall recognised from the title (`browser_open`) is judged again on the tab's current title (`tab list`) after each look, since the open call's title belongs to the wall.
- Limit: the actions return interactive-only snapshots, which carry little text, so a wall is most reliably caught on `browser_open` and `browser_snapshot`; this is in `docs/browser.md`.
- Login-wall detection requires a password field on a small page (1500 characters of visible text) or one headed or titled "Sign in", so a long content page with a login box in its sidebar is not a login wall.
- The granted/ungranted notes are tested in `browser/routines.test.ts` ("routine sign-in", chat-hosted and headless) through the real path: the routine's `browser_login` parks, **Allow** on the first-use card grants the entry, and the next run's `browser_open` of the sign-in page names `browser_login`. Rebased on ticket 05, whose `unattended` removal left `wallPauseMs` as the only addition to `BrowserToolContext`, `BrowserEndpointDeps` and `createBrowser`; the docs paragraph keeps 05's vault sign-in text and points its login-wall sentence at "Page walls".
